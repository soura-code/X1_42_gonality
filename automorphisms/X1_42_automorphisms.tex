This file accompanies the paper \emph{The gonality of $X_1(42)$ is $12$}
and records all twelve elements of $\Aut_{\F_5}(X)$, $X=X_1(42)_{\F_5}$,
as computed by Magma's \texttt{Automorphisms}.

\section*{Conventions}
Magma represents the function field of $X$ (built from Sutherland's
\texttt{FFFc42} model) as
\[
  \F_5(X)=\F_5(t)[y]/\bigl(F(t,y)\bigr),\qquad [\F_5(X):\F_5(t)]=16,
\]
where $t$ generates the rational subfield and $y$ is the generator
\texttt{AFF.1}. An automorphism $\sigma$ is determined by $\sigma(t)$ and
$\sigma(y)$, each written uniquely as $\sum_{j=0}^{15}c_j(t)\,y^j$ with
$c_j\in\F_5(t)$.

After cancellation, every denominator that occurs is of the form
$t^{a}(t+4)^{b}(t^2+4t+1)^{c}=t^{a}(t-1)^{b}(t^2-t+1)^{c}$. We write
$c_j=N_j(t)/\bigl(t^{a_j}(t+4)^{b_j}(t^2+4t+1)^{c_j}\bigr)$ and record, for
each $j$, the exponents $a_j,b_j,c_j$ and the coefficients of $N_j\in\F_5[t]$
from the leading coefficient down to the constant term; a row with entry $0$
means $c_j=0$. For example, a row $j\;|\;2\;1\;0\;|\;2\;|\;3\,0\,1$ stands for
$c_j=(3t^2+1)/\bigl(t^2(t+4)\bigr)$.

The group is cyclic of order $12$, generated by $\sigma_4$, and acts simply
transitively on the twelve rational places $P_1,\dots,P_{12}$ (numbered as in
Magma's \texttt{Places(AFF,1)}). Table~1 gives the order of each $\sigma_i$,
its expression as a power of $\sigma_4$, whether it lies in the diamond
subgroup $C$, and its permutation of the rational places; Table~2 lists the
orbits of each cyclic subgroup $\langle\sigma_i\rangle$ on the rational places.

$\{P_2,P_4,P_8,P_9,P_{11},P_{12}\}\ni Q_1$ and
$\{P_1,P_3,P_5,P_6,P_7,P_{10}\}\ni Q_2$.
